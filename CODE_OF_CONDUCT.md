# Code of Conduct

This project aims to provide a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background or identity. Participants are expected to communicate with respect, accept constructive feedback, and focus discussion on the work and its impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove or reject contributions and take other actions they consider appropriate to address unacceptable behavior.

This policy is based on the principles of [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
